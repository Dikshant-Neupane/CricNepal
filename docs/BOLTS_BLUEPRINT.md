# Janakpur Bolts — Season 3 blueprint

Standing analyst guidance for the season, derived from the project's phase priors
(S1 + S2 ball-by-ball plus each player's wider T20 history) and the TU Kirtipur
venue tables built from our own 64 matches. Suggestions, not instructions: every
line carries the number behind it, and priors are two NPL seasons of evidence —
they describe tendencies, not certainties. This blueprint is revised only at the
pre-registered checkpoints (after matches 10 and 20), alongside the model review.

## Batting order
- **Open with Anil Sah.** His powerplay rate (1.11 runs per ball) is the squad's
  best; almost everyone else sits at the league-average ~1.07. Accept a measured
  powerplay — TU powerplays run at ~6.3 an over league-wide — because this squad's
  edge is at the death, not the top.
- **Never open with Moeen Ali** (powerplay 0.91, his weakest phase by a distance).
  His batting value is the death (1.55); bat him 5-6 as a floater who can be held
  back for the last five overs.
- **Neesham is the middle-overs engine** (1.27 runs per ball in the middle phase,
  the squad's best anti-spin gear) — slots 4-5, timed to attack spin, which takes
  55-57% of bowler wickets at TU and dominates overs 7-14.
- **Iftikhar Ahmed is the stabiliser** (flat 1.04 / 1.08 / 1.45 across phases):
  the anchor to bat through a spin-heavy middle, not a powerplay gamble.
- **Keep Harmeet Singh a late slot** (death 1.50): finisher with the bat,
  spearhead with the ball — do not spend both roles early.

## Bowling plan
- **Harmeet owns the powerplay**: economy 5.89 in the phase, the best number in
  the squad — three powerplay overs, attacking fields, wickets over containment.
- **The spin trio owns the middle**: Moeen (middle economy 6.47), Lalit Rajbanshi
  (6.50), Iftikhar (6.74). At a venue where spin takes the majority of wickets,
  overs 7-14 are Janakpur's control phase.
- **Death: Moeen first** (8.08), then Iftikhar (8.52) / Bikash Aagri (8.51).
- **Keep Neesham out of overs 17-20** (death economy 9.53, his clear weakness);
  his bowling value is overs 5-14 (middle 6.91). He is a bat-first all-rounder
  in this squad's optimal usage.

## Selection principles
- All four overseas players (Harmeet, Neesham, Moeen, Iftikhar) are nailed on
  when available — and Harmeet (USA) satisfies the Associate-player XI clause,
  if it is in force, at zero selection cost. Other teams pay a slot for that.
- The real contest is the last three Nepali spots. Prefer the experienced floor
  (Tiwari, Rit Gautam) over debutants on default priors: an expected-XI selector
  cannot tell a debutant from an average player, a coach should not pretend
  otherwise either. A debutant plays when training/availability forces or earns it.

## Venue targets (TU Kirtipur, from our 64 matches)
- Par is the **low 150s** (first-innings mean 147.5, median 144.5).
- Batting first, **155+ is a strong position**: chase success falls to ~33% in
  the 150-159 band and ~25% at 160-169.
- Chasing under 150, Janakpur are historically favoured (58-80% success in the
  130-149 bands): pace the chase, wickets in hand beat early fireworks —
  which suits this squad's death-heavy batting profile anyway.
- The 17:00 day-night slot reads higher (first-innings mean 160.3) but on only
  9 matches — treat as indicative, and log the dew either way.

## What this blueprint is not
- Not a prediction. Probabilities live on the Match Predictions page and in the
  frozen locks; this page is decisions only.
- Not current-form analysis. Per-match form (last-10-T20s) rides in each
  opponent card's form watch, refreshed at the T-48h sweep before every match.
