## Our plan in one paragraph
This is a must-win, last-league-game plan, and the prescription from Match 21
is the powerplay of the chase: the Bolts lost Match 21 by 4.4 overs (25/3, the
top three making 13 between them), so Friday's fix is the openers surviving —
Anil Sah opens (the blueprint) and whoever opens with him must see off Rijan
(PP 6.780 on 120 balls) and Ranjit (PP 6.449) rather than trade early wickets
with them, because Chitwan's attack defends par by taking wickets in clusters
(their powerplay takes 2.18 wickets a match in S1–S2, and Biratnagar lost their
last five for 17 in Match 2). With the bat, rotate off Raza (middle 7.088 on
1172.5 balls) and Ranjit (middle 6.546) through overs 7–14, and target Mousley
(Season 3 9.86 an over) and Malla (19.50 in Match 20, five wides) instead;
respect Airee and Rijan on Season 3 evidence (9 and 10 wickets at 5.80 and
6.75), not on their priors. With the ball, take the Mishara matchup early and
aggressively: Harmeet Singh's powerplay economy on our priors is 5.89, the best
powerplay number on either side — bowl him at Mishara while the field is up,
wickets over containment, because Mishara's death number (1.5433) and his LPL
form punish survival. Hold the middle for Raza's entry and do not let Raza
(death 1.6051, 106 not out a fortnight ago) or Bopara (death 1.5116, 55 off 35
in Match 20) be set at over 17, because the death pair without Moeen is Harmeet
himself (death 8.27 on our priors) and Bikash Aagri (8.51, not in the Match 21
XI) — name that pair before the innings starts, keep Neesham (death 9.53) out
of overs 17–20 entirely, and if Tector plays (re-pin permitting) his 4-0-9-1 on
debut is the middle-overs control option. Open with Anil Sah and float Neesham
at 4–5 to attack the spin through the middle (his middle 1.27 on the blueprint
is our best anti-spin gear); if Burl plays, he is the Moeen batting replacement
at 5–6 (death 1.5663 on his prior) and the finish falls to him, Saif Hassan and
Harmeet himself, so a set batter batting through is worth more than a late
assault that never arrives. And fix the calling between the wickets: two
run-outs in six balls in Match 21 is a fielding and calling problem, not a
bowling problem. Janakpur's Associate-in-XI, if the clause is real: Harmeet
Singh (USA). The overseas-slot questions for Friday (Malan recalled or not,
