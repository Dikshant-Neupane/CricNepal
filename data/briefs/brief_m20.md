## How Kathmandu approach games (S1–S2 evidence, tested today)

- **A bat-first total 53 above their average, built on one rescue
  stand and a fast finish.** S1–S2 batting first Kathmandu average
  126.6 and win 44.4% of those games (9 games). Today, after losing
  the toss and being put in, they made 180/8. The powerplay was 42/2
  against a profile 38.9 runs and 2.53 wickets — three runs above, half
  a wicket better — and both openers were gone by 3.5 overs: David
  Warner 1 off 6 (0.17 against a 1.2504 prior) and Sahil Patel 8 off
  12 (0.67 against 1.1352), the side 13/2.
- **The rescue was Rashid Khan, and it was miles above his prior.**
  Rashid made 52 off 30 (1.73 against a 1.0404 prior), two fours and
  four sixes — his second fifty of the tournament. He put on 62 off
  34 balls with William Bosisto, 27 off 22 (no prior on file), and 53

  Karan KC made 20 off 7 (2.86 against 1.2946), three sixes, and
  Santosh Yadav finished 8 not out off 2 (4.00 against 1.1728).
- **The finish was the highest gear of the innings.** The last five
  overs went for 48 runs and four wickets against a profile death
  average of 30.9 runs per match — well above what Kathmandu usually
  manage at the death.
- **The bowling was two tight spells, two leaks, and 8 wides — and
  the margin absorbed all of it.** S1–S2 bowling in the powerplay
  Kathmandu concede 40.1 runs and take 2.06 wickets per match. Today
