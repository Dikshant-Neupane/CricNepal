## What it means for the Janakpur Bolts (Match 25, Fri 13 Nov, 17:00 v Chitwan)

1. **Friday is now a must-win, and it is the last league game.**
   Janakpur play their seventh and final league match on Friday
   against Chitwan, who sit on 5 points — one ahead. Win, and
   Janakpur go to 6 and can still reach the playoffs; lose, and they
   finish on 4 points and the season ends in the league stage. There
   is no game after Friday to repair this one.
2. **The prescription flips from the death to the powerplay.** The
   M20 brief said the game was lost in the death, not the powerplay,
   and the prescription was death bowling. Tonight the chase was
   lost by 4.4 overs: 25/3, with the top three making 13 between
   them. For Friday, the fix is the first six overs of the chase —
   the openers surviving, not the death bowlers finishing. The
   blueprint still says Anil Sah opens; the question is who opens
   with him, after Mahata's 10 off 11 tonight and Emon's 1 in
   Match 14.
3. **The captain's bowling cannot keep covering the batting.**
   Harmeet has now taken 4 for 17 and still lost by 27, because the
   chase never got past 47/6. In Matches 14 and 21 the pattern is
   the same: the bowling holds a side to a chaseable total and the
   batting does not chase it. Friday needs the batting to do its
   half — 121 was chaseable, and the side made 93.
4. **Two run-outs in six balls is a fielding and calling problem,
   not a bowling problem.** Das was the set batter and the top
   scorer when he was run out at 46/5; Shusling followed at 47/6.
   Both involved Bhandari behind the stumps, which is the
   opposition doing its job — the calling between the wickets is
   Janakpur's to fix before Friday.
5. **The favourite won again — and the lock is now 2–3 over the
   last five.** The P2 favourite lost Matches 17, 18 and 19, then
   won Matches 20 and 21, both times as a narrow favourite (0.5039
   and 0.5267). The match-20 checkpoint's call stands: no promotion,
   no refit, and the toss/chase adjustment is neither demonstrably
   helping nor shown to be harm. Tonight the simulator was the most
   confident voice (0.6265) and it was right.
