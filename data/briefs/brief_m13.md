## What it means for the Janakpur Bolts (Match 14, Thu 5 Nov, 12:30)

1. **Survive Trumpelmann's new-ball spell, or repeat Pokhara.**
   Pokhara's game ended at 15/3 in 3.5 overs; the Bolts' blueprint
   opens with Anil Sah and prizes the powerplay. The first six
   overs on Thursday are the match: Lumbini's powerplay bowling
   has now taken 3 wickets for 23 in a live Season 3 match, with
   Trumpelmann striking in the first over. A Bolts powerplay that
   loses three early, as Pokhara's did, leaves a chase or a
   defence to be built from behind for the remaining 14 overs.
2. **Paudel is the wicket, twice over.** He finished this chase

   cannot treat him as a containing bowler to milk — his one over

   bowl at loosely in the middle overs.

   band is the low 150s. Thursday is a 12:30 day game, not an
   evening game, so the evening dew note in the capture pack does
   not apply — but a first-innings total in the 150s is still the
   target the Bolts should be planning around, not the 97
   Lumbini just chased down.
4. **The Moeen question stands, on the card's reading.** The
   Match 14 card records Moeen Ali's four-match stint (Matches 3,
