## How Sudurpaschim approached it

Batting first against their profile, and it went wrong from the
first ball. Sudurpaschim's bat-first average across the profile's
fourteen matches is 151.3, and they win 71.4% of the matches they
bat first in; tonight they made 105 — 46 below that average, their
fifth sub-120 total in the profile's 22 matches. The powerplay went
20 for 3, against a profile of 43.7 runs and 1.73 wickets — 24 runs
short, and the lowest powerplay total of the season so far. George
Munsey, signed the day before and playing his first match for the
Royals, was bowled by Ruben Trumpelmann with the first ball of the
match: a golden duck against a prior of 1.4104 runs per ball, the
highest bat prior in the Sudurpaschim squad. Thanuka Dabare (3 off
8) and Binod Bhandari (3 off 9) followed inside four overs, and at
the halfway mark Sudurpaschim had 40 for 3.

The innings was one stand. Dipendra Singh Airee and Ishan Pandey
added 64 for the fourth wicket (8 for 3 to 72 for 4), and no other
batter reached double figures. Airee's 52 off 51 (three fours, one
six — the only six of the Sudurpaschim innings, a reverse sweep off
Malla in the 18th over) came at 1.02 runs per ball against a prior
of 1.382, so even the captain scored well below his own rate; he was
the only batter to reach fifty. Pandey's 27 off 33 (three fours)
came at 0.82 against a prior of 1.1094, and he was run out by a
Dickwella direct hit — one of two run outs in the innings, both
involving Dickwella (Kuggeleijn, 7 off 5, was run out by Ali Sheikh
and Dickwella). After Pandey fell at 72 for 4 in the 15th over,
Sudurpaschim added 33 for 4: Ratnayake bowled by Malla for 1, Aarif
Sheikh caught by Short off Malla for 4, Airee caught by Short off
Mahato for 52, Kuggeleijn run out. After Pandey fell at 72 for 4 in the
15th over, Sudurpaschim added only 33 for 4 in the remainder of the
innings, and the closing overs never recovered the base the top
order had lost. Anil Kharel finished 2 not out; Bohara and Dhami
did not bat.

## How Lumbini approached it

Won the toss, fielded as usual, and bowled Sudurpaschim out of the
game before the chase began. Trumpelmann's opening spell set it:
4-0-14-2 at 3.50 an over against a prior economy of 8.333, including
Munsey first ball and Dabare. Gautam was the most economical bowler
on either side, 4-1-11-1 at 2.75 against a prior of 6.562, with a
maiden. Malla took 2 for 27 at 6.75, right on his prior of 6.817,
and Mahato 1 for 19 at 4.75 against a prior of 7.315. The only
expensive overs came from part-time: Ali Sheikh 2-0-20-0 at 10.00
and Short 2-0-12-0. Lumbini's powerplay bowling conceded 20,
against a profile of 39.1 runs conceded per match.

The chase was one man. Niroshan Dickwella's unbeaten 59 off 37
(seven fours, two sixes) came at 1.59 runs per ball against a prior
of 1.1893 — 55% of Lumbini's total — and he was there at the end,
hitting the winning six off Dabare in the 17th over. Every other
Lumbini batter scored below his prior: Short 0 off 3 (prior 1.1684),
Aasif Sheikh 4 off 8 (prior 1.2998), Rohit Paudel 21 off 28 at 0.75
against a prior of 1.2434, Sundeep Jora 8 off 13 (prior 1.2375),
Dilip Nath 10 not out off 12 (prior 1.1068). The powerplay went 38
for 2 against a profile of 43.9 runs and 1.79 wickets, and the
Dickwella–Paudel stand of 38 was the biggest of the chase. Against
a target of 106 none of that mattered: Lumbini's chase average in
the profile is 121.7, and they got home with 20 balls to spare.
Sudurpaschim's bowling had one bright spot — Hemant Dhami 3-1-12-1
at 4.00 against a prior economy of 7.5, and Ratnayake 2 for 25 at
6.25 against a prior of 7.026 — but Kuggeleijn went for 22 in two
overs (11.00 against a prior of 6.84), and Airee's 4-0-23-0 could
not buy the breakthrough.

## What it means for the Bolts

Nothing to prepare for, and that is the point to record plainly.
Janakpur finished the league stage seventh on 4 points and are
eliminated; there are no Bolts fixtures left in the season, and
none of the three remaining matches involves them. The tournament
runs on without them: Eliminator tomorrow (Kathmandu v Chitwan),
Qualifier 2 on Thursday (Sudurpaschim v the Eliminator winner),
final on Saturday (Lumbini v the Qualifier 2 winner). What tonight
showed, for the record, is the gap the Bolts never closed: the two
finalists both defend small totals with new-ball economy — Lumbini
held Sudurpaschim to 105 with Trumpelmann at 3.50 and Gautam at
2.75 — and both can win a chase on one batter's shoulders, as
Dickwella did with 59 of 108. The Bolts' season ended in the league
stage; the remaining work for this project is the Eliminator and
Qualifier 2 captures, then the final.
