## The one-line read

A captaincy change that changed nothing — Miller's Karnali folded
for 105 after winning the toss and batting — and a chase that
wobbled to 19 for 3 before Short's 46 and an unbeaten 28 from Dilip
Nath closed it with 20 balls left.

## What it means for the Janakpur Bolts (Match 25, Fri 13 Nov, 17:00 v Chitwan)

1. **Friday is still a must-win, and the top of the table is now
   settled.** Lumbini's win seals their playoff place on 9 points.
   A Janakpur win on Friday takes them to 6, level with Biratnagar,
   so the playoff race then turns on the remaining results and net
   run rate. Lose, and Janakpur finish on 4 points and the season
   ends in the league stage.
2. **A change at the top does not fix a top order.** Karnali changed
   captains — Miller for Sompal — and lost by the same shape, 105
   all out with the top four making 13 between them. Friday's XI
   should be picked on form, not on a gesture.
3. **The chase lesson cuts both ways.** Lumbini were 19 for 3
   chasing 106 and still won with 20 balls spare, because Short
   (46) and Nath (28 not out) rebuilt. Janakpur's chases have died
   at exactly that kind of start — 47 for 6 in Match 21. Friday
   needs a rebuild pair, not just a fast start.
4. **A brilliant spell in a losing cause is the same story twice.**
   Mark Watt took 4 for 12 and still lost by four wickets, because
   his side made 105. Harmeet Singh did the same for Janakpur in
   Match 21 (4 for 17) and still lost by 27, because the batting
   made 93. The bowling is already doing its half; Friday is a
   batting test.
5. **The favourite won again — and the lock is now 4–3 over the last
   seven.** The P2 favourite lost Matches 17, 18 and 19, then won
   Matches 20, 21, 22 and 23, all four times as a narrow favourite
   (0.5039, 0.5267, 0.535, 0.5547). The match-20 checkpoint's call
   stands: no promotion, no refit. The simulator got this one right
   too (Lumbini 0.5967).
