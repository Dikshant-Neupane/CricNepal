## How Chitwan approached it

Batted first after winning the toss and produced the third-highest
total of the season, behind Pokhara's 224 and 217. Chitwan's bat-first average across the
profile's ten bat-first matches is 153.3; tonight they made 204,
51 above it. The start went against them: Karan KC took two wickets
in two balls in the fourth over (Dev Khanal 17 off 15, then Dawid
Malan first ball, caught Bosisto) and Chitwan were 22 for 2. The
recovery was one stand. Kamil Mishara and Ravi Bopara added 154
off 91 balls for the third wicket, from 22 for 2 to 176 for 3.

Mishara's 120 not out off 59 (five fours, ten sixes) came at 2.03
runs per ball against a prior of 1.3184 — the highest individual
score in NPL history, passing Mark Watt's 114, and the season's
second century. Bopara's 53 off 42 came at 1.26 against a prior of
1.2554, exactly on his number, and he was the anchor Mishara batted
around. After Bopara fell at 176 for 3 in the 19th over, Bibek
Kumar Yadav made 8 off 3 and the last over produced 20 runs,
Mishara finishing unbeaten with Kushal Malla. Kathmandu's bowling
had two wicket-takers and no control: Karan KC 2 for 32 and Rashid
Khan 2 for 38, but the support offered nothing — Sonu Devkota's
single over cost 27, Ganesh Gautam went for 31 in three overs,
Santosh Yadav 21 in two and Jahandad Khan 34 in four.

## How Kathmandu approached it

The chase profile said this was never their game. Kathmandu's
chase average across the profile's eight chases is 136.8, and the
target was 205. The powerplay went 36 for 2, close to their profile
of 38.9 runs and 2.53 wickets, but the required rate climbed from
the start and no partnership passed 33. At 63 for 3 (Bosisto, 37
off 28 at 1.32 against a prior of 1.1674) the chase
became a collapse: 67 for 4, 91 for 5, 92 for 6, 94 for 7.

The damage was done by two bowlers. Rijan Dhakal took 4 for 16 at
4.00 an over against a prior economy of 7.478, and Usama Mir 3 for
15 at 3.75 against a prior of 6.861 — seven wickets for 31 runs in
eight overs between them. Mir's line closes his availability arc:
NOC-blocked through the league stage, cleared in time to debut in
Match 27, and now a match-shaping spell in the Eliminator. Kamal
Singh Airee bowled 4-0-16-0 at the top. Bhim Sharki's unbeaten 30
off 17 (three sixes) was the only late resistance, and Kathmandu's
143 was above their chase average and still 61 short.

league stage seventh on 4 points and are eliminated; the
