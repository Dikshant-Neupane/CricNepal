## The one-line read

A chase won in the last three overs after it had nearly been lost in
the middle — Lumbini were 86 for 1 at 11.5 overs, then 98 for 4 two
overs later, and Sundeep Jora's 25 off 11 dragged them home off the
last ball.

  were 33 for 1 at 4.4 overs: Lokesh Bam caught for 15 off 17

## How Lumbini approach games (S1–S2 evidence, tested today)

- **A chase far above their average — and a win, as usual.** S1–S2
  chasing Lumbini average 121.7 and win 61.5% of those games (13
  games). Today they chased 167, 45 above that average, and won by
  4 wickets with a ball to spare. Winning the toss and fielding is
  their habit: S1–S2 they field first in 90.0% of the tosses they
  win, and they did so again tonight.
- **The powerplay was won on wickets, not runs.** S1–S2 batting in
  the powerplay Lumbini average 43.9 runs and lose 1.79 wickets per
  match. Today they were 39 for 0 at six overs — runs just below
  profile, wickets better than it. D'Arcy Short made 45 off 38
  (1.18 against a 1.1684 prior — exactly at his rate) and Aasif
  Sheikh 45 off 41 (1.10 against a 1.2998 prior): an 86-run opening
  stand in 71 balls.
- **The middle collapsed, then Jora finished it.** From 86 for 1 at
  11.5 overs Lumbini fell to 98 for 4 at 13.5 — 12 runs for three
  wickets in 12 balls: Niroshan Dickwella caught for 0 off 2
  (0.00 against a 1.1893 prior), Dilip Nath caught for 0 off 1

1. **The season is over, and the table has a new leader.** Janakpur

   Tonight's result matters only to the teams still playing: Lumbini
   go top on 11, and with two league matches left nobody can catch
   them — Kathmandu can reach 10 at best, Chitwan 9.

   Raj Bhandari, Ajay Chauhan, Dinesh Adhikari and Arniko Prasad
   Yadav;
   Trumpelmann, Mahato, Dilip Nath and Ali Sheikh came in. The
   XIs that actually played were settled at the toss, not in

3. **The lesson is the middle, again.** Both innings turned on a
   middle-overs swing: Biratnagar added 112 for one wicket between
   overs 5 and 18 and then stalled, 21 for two off the last 14
   balls; Lumbini lost three for 12 in two overs and were rescued
   by one finisher batting at nearly twice his prior. Janakpur's
   season died the same way in Match 25 — a middle that could not
   score, 4 off 26 from Mahata — and tonight is the other side of
   that coin: Jora's 25 off 11 is what a middle that can score
   looks like.
4. **Nothing left to play for, and nothing left to learn from the
   table.** Janakpur's last fixture is behind them. The two Sunday
   matches decide only the order below Lumbini: Kathmandu v Pokhara
   and Chitwan v Karnali settle second, third and fourth.
