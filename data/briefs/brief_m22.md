# Match 22 post-match brief — what Biratnagar v Pokhara tells the Janakpur Bolts

Prepared 12 Nov 2026, the afternoon of the match. Source: Cricbuzz
full scorecard, cross-checked against the FairPlay/Sportmonks full
scorecard and the Kathmandu Post match report. The sources agree on
both totals, the result, every batting line and every bowling line.
Two secondary reports differ on Lamichhane's figures — NTV World
gives 6 for 26 and an OnlineKhabar live blog gave 6 for 23 in three
overs mid-spell — but both full scorecards and the Kathmandu Post
match report give 6 for 27 in four overs, which is what is
transcribed here. Player of the Match: Sandeep Lamichhane
(FairPlay/Sportmonks). Toss: Pokhara Avengers won it and elected to
bat. The P2 post-toss lock had Biratnagar at 0.535 — Biratnagar
favourite; the pre-season P1 line had Biratnagar favourite at
0.5112, while the simulator favoured Pokhara at 0.5955 (sim mean
scores 139.0 vs 146.3). The P2 favourite won, the third straight
favourite win after Matches 20 and 21 ended the three-match run of
favourite losses.

**Result:** Biratnagar Kings won by 4 wickets. Pokhara Avengers 132
all out in 17.5 overs; Biratnagar Kings 136 for 6 in 19.2 overs,
chasing 133. Biratnagar go to 6 points (three wins, three defeats)
and fourth in the table; Pokhara stay on 2 points (one win, five
defeats) and are eliminated — the first team out of playoff
contention. The table now reads: Kathmandu 8, Sudurpaschim 8,
Lumbini 7, Biratnagar 6, Chitwan 5, Karnali 4, Janakpur 4, Pokhara 2.

## The one-line read

A one-man innings — Samra's 84 was 64% of Pokhara's 132 — undone by
a captain's six-wicket haul including a hat-trick, and then a chase
that wobbled to 105 for 6 before the seventh-wicket pair finished it
with four balls to spare.

## How Pokhara approach games (S1–S2 evidence, tested today)

- **A bat-first total 9 below their average — and a loss, as usual.**
  S1–S2 batting first Pokhara average 140.9 and win 25.0% of those
  games (8 games). Today they made 132 after winning the toss and
  batting, and lost.
- **Two wickets inside two overs, and the top order never recovered.**
  S1–S2 batting in the powerplay Pokhara average 44.9 runs and lose
  1.88 wickets per match. Today they were 11 for 2 in 1.6 overs:
  Arjun Kumal caught for 1 off 3 (0.33 against a 1.1044 prior) and
  Kushal Bhurtel lbw for a duck off 4 balls (0.00 against a 1.4606
  prior) — the biggest miss of the day against a prior. They reached
  only 46 for 3 in 6.2 overs.
- **One batter above his prior, and he made 64% of the total.**
  Yuvraj Samra made 84 off 47 (1.79 against a 1.4525 prior), seven
  fours and five sixes — his second straight big innings after 102
  off 48 against Karnali in Match 19. The next best was Dhananjaya
  Lakshan, 22 off 15 (1.47 against a 1.1859 prior). Nobody else
  passed 12: Sunny BK made 12 off 11 (1.09 against a 1.1731 prior),
  Arjun Gharti 5 off 10 (0.50 against a 1.1128 prior), Bipin Khatri
  5 off 12 (0.42 against a 1.1471 prior), and there were four ducks
  — Bhurtel, Ramesh Mendis, Chamika Karunaratne and Kiran Thagunna.
- **The collapse was the story of the innings.** From 122 for 5 in
  the 15th over, Pokhara lost their final five wickets for 10 runs
  and were all out for 132. Lamichhane took three of them off
  consecutive balls — Samra at 14.3, Karunaratne at 14.4, Thagunna
  at 14.5 — the first hat-trick of Season 3. S1–S2 batting at the
  death Pokhara average 33.6 runs per match; today, from 122 for 5,
  they made 10.
- **The bowling kept the chase honest, then leaked at the end.**
  S1–S2 bowling in the powerplay Pokhara concede 46.2 runs and take
  1.71 wickets per match; today Biratnagar were 39 for 2 at six
  overs — runs below profile, wickets on it. Kushal Bhurtel took
  4-1-21-2 (5.25 against a 7.569 prior) and Ramesh Mendis 4-0-29-2
  (7.25, no prior on file), but Dhananjaya Lakshan went for
  3.2-0-35-1 (10.50 against a 6.814 prior), the most expensive spell
  of the match, and Sagar Dhakal's 4-0-22-0 (5.50 against a 7.485
  prior) took no wicket. Five wides in the innings.

## How Biratnagar approach games (S1–S2 evidence, tested today)

- **A chase total above their average — and a win, against the
  profile.** S1–S2 chasing Biratnagar average 130.6 and win 40.0%
  of those games (10 games). Today they chased 133 and won, with
  four balls to spare.
- **The powerplay was lost on runs, not wickets.** S1–S2 batting in
  the powerplay Biratnagar average 46.0 runs and lose 1.95 wickets
  per match. Today they were 39 for 2 at six overs: Imran Sheikh
  caught for 10 off 9 (1.11 against a 1.1555 prior) and Charith
  Asalanka caught for a duck off 3 balls (0.00 against a 1.2237
  prior).
- **The middle wobbled, and the top scorer was below his prior.**
  From 56 for 3 at 9.1 overs Biratnagar slid to 77 for 4 at 12.6,
  78 for 5 at 13.3 and 105 for 6 at 16.5. Basir Ahamad top-scored
  with 37 off 39 (0.95 against a 1.2234 prior), Lokesh Bam made 33
  off 27 (1.22 against a 1.34 prior), Angelo Mathews 8 off 11 (0.73
  against a 1.1399 prior) and Pratis GC a duck off 1 ball (0.00
  against a 1.2376 prior). Four of the top six below their priors.
- **The seventh-wicket pair finished it.** From 105 for 6, Shehan
  Jayasuriya, 17 not out off 14 (1.21 against a 1.113 prior), and
  Isuru Udana, 25 not out off 12 (2.08, no prior on file), put on
  31 off 15 balls. S1–S2 batting at the death Biratnagar average
  34.2 runs per match; the pair's 31 off 15 is the shape that wins
  chases.
- **The bowling won the game before the chase started.** S1–S2
  bowling in the powerplay Biratnagar concede 46.8 runs and take
  1.9 wickets per match; today they had Pokhara 11 for 2 in 1.6
  overs. Sandeep Lamichhane took 4-0-27-6 (6.75 against a 6.461
  prior) — the economy a touch above his prior, but six wickets,
  after taking just one wicket in his previous five matches. It was
  the first five-wicket haul of the season, the first hat-trick of
  Season 3, and the second-best figures in NPL history (behind
  Shahab Alam's 6 for 25). Angelo Mathews took 3-0-22-1 (7.33
  against a 7.126 prior), Charith Asalanka 4-0-28-1 (7.00 against a
  7.196 prior) and Rupesh Singh 0.5-0-2-1 (2.40 against a 7.867
  prior) with the last wicket. The leak was Shehan Jayasuriya,
  3-0-32-0 (10.67 against a 7.04 prior). Two wides in the innings.

## What it means for the Janakpur Bolts (Match 25, Fri 13 Nov, 17:00 v Chitwan)

1. **Friday is still a must-win, and Biratnagar's win has raised the
   bar.** Biratnagar now sit fourth on 6 points. A Janakpur win on
   Friday takes them to 6 as well, level with Biratnagar, so the
   playoff race then turns on the remaining results and net run
   rate. Lose, and Janakpur finish on 4 points and the season ends
   in the league stage.
2. **The one-man-team warning.** Pokhara had the innings of the day
   — Samra 84, 64% of 132 — and lost with four balls to spare,
   because nobody else passed 22. Janakpur's batting has leaned the
   same way in Matches 14 and 21; Friday needs two or three
   contributors, not one anchor.
3. **The chase lesson cuts both ways.** Biratnagar's chase wobbled
   to 105 for 6 and was rescued by the seventh-wicket pair's 31 off
   15. Janakpur's chases have died exactly there — 47 for 6 in
   Match 21. Friday's lower order has to be ready to finish, not
   just the top four to start.
4. **A captain's spell can rescue a side — but only half the job.**
   Lamichhane's 6 for 27 turned 122 for 5 into 132 all out.
   Harmeet Singh did the same for Janakpur in Match 21 (4 for 17)
   and still lost by 27, because the batting made 93. The bowling
   is already doing its half; Friday is a batting test.
5. **The favourite won again — and the lock is now 3–3 over the last
   six.** The P2 favourite lost Matches 17, 18 and 19, then won
   Matches 20, 21 and 22, all three times as a narrow favourite
   (0.5039, 0.5267, 0.535). The match-20 checkpoint's call stands:
   no promotion, no refit. The simulator got this one wrong
   (Pokhara 0.5955) while the P2 line was right.
