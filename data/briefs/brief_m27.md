## The one-line read

A dead rubber that played like one for everyone except Hussain
Talat: 51 not out off 33 and 3 for 17, the innings' top score and
the only three-wicket haul on either side, in a game where both
middles stalled and both attacks leaked.

  batting, they made 153 for 8 — 10 above the average — and won.

  S1–S2 batting in the powerplay Karnali average 41.6 runs and lose
  1.84 wickets per match. Today they were 46 for 3 at six overs:
  runs above profile, wickets worse than it. Sachin Sharma was lbw
  for 0 off 3 (0.00 against a 1.1731 prior), Ariful Islam made 35
  off 15 (2.33 against a 1.1102 prior — 2.10 times his rate) and
  Deepak Dumre was bowled for 7 off 10 (0.70 against a 1.1561

## What it means for the Janakpur Bolts

1. **The season is over, and this match changes nothing for
   Janakpur.** Janakpur finish on 4 points, two wins from six,
   eliminated since Match 25. Tonight's result matters only to the
   teams still playing: Karnali's win lifts them to sixth on 6
   points, and Chitwan stay fourth on 7 whatever happens in Match

2. **The pre-season expected XIs did not happen, on either side.**
   Chitwan left out Ravi Bopara, Kaleem Sana, Ranjeet Kumar,
   Mousom Dhakal, Arjun Saud and Bipin Rawal from the pre-season
   expected XI; Kamil Mishara, Dan Mousley, Dev Khanal, Kamal Singh
   Airee, Rijan Dhakal and Usama Mir came in. Karnali left out

   Tamang; Ariful Islam, David Miller, Hussain Talat and Nandan
   Yadav came in. The XIs that actually played were settled at the
   toss, not in October.
3. **The lesson is the same one as Match 26: one player at twice
   his rate decides a game both middles lose.** Karnali's middle
   scored 62 for four in 11.4 overs and Chitwan's scored 42 for
   four in 7.4 — both below their priors almost man for man — and
   the match turned on Talat batting at 1.37 times his prior and
   then taking 3 for 17 at 5.37 against a 7.369 prior. Janakpur's
   season died the same way in Match 25 — a middle that could not
   score — and tonight is the other side of that coin: a side
   whose middle stalled still won because one all-rounder did both
   jobs.
4. **Nothing left to play for, and nothing left to learn from the
   table.** Janakpur's last fixture is behind them. Match 28
   tonight, Kathmandu v Pokhara, decides only third place.
